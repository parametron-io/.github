# Code of Conduct

Parametron aims to provide a professional, welcoming, and technically constructive environment for contributors and users.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- treat others with respect and good faith
- critique ideas and code without attacking people
- give technical feedback that is specific and actionable
- respect differences in experience, background, and communication style
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and deliberately disruptive behavior
- respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, sustained personal attacks, doxxing, and other behavior that makes participation unsafe or hostile are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in Parametron repositories, issue and pull-request discussions, organization-managed community spaces, and other project interactions when a participant is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other material that violates this policy and may restrict participation when necessary.

Conduct concerns should be reported privately to [info@parametron.io](mailto:info@parametron.io). Please include enough context for the report to be evaluated without posting sensitive details publicly.

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.
